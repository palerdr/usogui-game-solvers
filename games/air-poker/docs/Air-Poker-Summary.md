Poker Explained
You don't need to have a super complicated understanding of poker and a bunch of 200 IQ strategies. Poker is pretty simple and having just a basic understanding of how it works will be enough. You might have even gotten the gist of it through reading the Air Poker arc itself, without any prior knowledge. Even though I tried my best to make the explanation simple, it's quite lengthy. So if you're not interested in reading this section you can fully skip it and go to the Air Poker overview directly.

Each player holds 5 cards which only they can see. These 5 cards form what is called a poker hand, and its strength is determined by the poker hierarchy. You don't need to know every single possible hand. At most you should know the three strongest hands:

Royal Flush: Ace, 10, jack, queen and king of the same suit.

Example: A♦️ 10♦️ J♦️ Q♦️ K♦️

2. Straight flush: Five cards in a continuous sequence and of the same suit.

Example: 2♣️ 3♣️ 4♣️ 5♣️ 6♣️

3. 4-of-a-kind: Consists of four cards of one rank and one more of another rank.

Example: 4♠️ 4♣️ 4♦️ 4♥️ 9♦️

Other than that, between two of the same type of hands, the stronger one is the one with the higher cards.

Example: (8♦️ 8♥️ 8♣️ 8♠️ 2♠️) is stronger than (3♣️ 3♠️ 3♦️ 3♥️ 6♣️) since 8 > 3. It's not too important to know every possible tie breaker but just assume in a general sense that if there are two of the same hands, the one with the higher number is usually stronger.

How Poker is played:

Each player holds a poker hand. And not knowing what the other players hands are, they have to make their decisions based off of how strong their hand is, and how strong they think the others' hands are. One of the players definitely do have the stronger hand, it's just a matter of if they can translate that hand into a victory which they will have to do through mind games with the opponents by using one of the possible actions.

In a round of poker the players can decide between a few actions to take:

Betting/Raising: You put some of your chips into the pot, increasing the amount you earn if you win.

Calling: You match the previous player's bet. If they bet 3 chips, calling would mean you place 3 chips as well. You also can not bet less than the previous player. If they bet 6 chips, you have to either match it or bet more. You can not bet 4 chips for example. The exception is if you do not have enough chips to match, in which the only bet you can make is all-in.

Checking: You pass the actions to the next player, ending your turn without betting. You can only check if nobody else has placed a bet in this round

Folding: You give up the round. Once you fold, you are out and can not possibly win the round, even if your hand happens to be the strongest one.

These are the actions the player can decide between when playing poker. There are two other things you should know.

Ante: This is a forced bet each player has to place before the start of each round. So if you have a weak hand, you can't escape playing without losing a few chips. It ensures that you still end up paying something even if you fold.

When the round ends: This confused me when reading since I don't think it was mentioned anywhere. But a round of poker ends when all remaining players' bet are the same. Say for example that there are 5 players, 2 have folded, 2 have bet 3 chips each. If the last player now bets 3 chips, the remaining players have bet the same amount of chips. The round therefore ends, the players reveal their hands (except the ones who folded). If 4 players fold then there is only one remaining player and he wins automatically.

That's pretty much it. I know it's too long to be a brief and quick guide. But maybe you know most of it anyway. I just wanted to be thorough. It also helps for those who know nothing about poker, if there are any. Anyway, moving on to the arc.

The law and its clues:

As we know, the number on the cards represent a poker hand matching the sum of that number. For example, the number 15 can be turned into a straight flush: Ace, 2 3 4 and 5 of the same suit because 1 (ace) + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 15.

And there are a few clues that Baku, Lalo and Kaji picked up when analysing the game:

1. The range of the cards being between 8 and 63 (45 from Baku's perspective). The smallest number a hand can make is 6, a 4 of a kind of aces and a 2. The highest number a hand can make is 64, a 4 of a kind of kings and a queen. All numbers Baku know of fall into this range, between 6 and 64.

2. The most revealing one, why there are 5 rounds. One metal card = one poker hand. Poker hand = 5 cards. 1 metal card uses up 5 cards in creating a poker hand, 10 metal cards would use up 50. And there are 52 cards in a deck, not enough to create a hand for an 11th metal card.

3. The sum of Baku's cards is close 182, half of 364 which is the sum of a deck. In other words, Baku holds half of the metal cards and also half of a normal deck. Baku's 5 cards = half a deck, suggesting that all 10 cards = full deck.
